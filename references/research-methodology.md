# Research methodology

## Evidence, confidence and market state

Facts carry source URLs, instrument, units, UTC observation time, retrieval time and quality flags. Findings cite evidence IDs. A data point reused by macro and Fed actors is one observation, not two independent votes. Separate facts, conditional hypotheses, correlations and causal claims.

Full research examines 1D, 1W and 1M confirmed UTC bars. Monthly data needs enough history for the requested indicator; warm-up shortfalls remain missing. 4H is an optional execution-context view. Never interpolate monthly bars into a stronger trend claim or mix swap and spot observations without labels.

A research score aggregates declared evidence weights only. It is not a measured success probability. Directional confidence is a qualitative evidence assessment; historical win rate is an empirical fraction under a specified experiment; estimated probability requires calibration and out-of-sample evaluation.

## Macro and Fed

Distinguish observations, prior observations, revisions, market consensus, policy outcomes and surprises. Missing consensus means surprise is unknown. FRED graph CSV current series are generally revised/latest vintage, not point-in-time vintage. Without release vintages, report descriptive history rather than a macro surprise strategy backtest.

Fed rate cuts are conditional events: recession risk, inflation, expectations, positioning and liquidity can change their market meaning. Official statements/RSS establish dates and content; the researcher must state whether rate expectations or futures pricing are unavailable. Do not infer an exact policy probability from prose.

## Strategy and review

Construct independently justified long, short and wait scenarios. Each candidate identifies triggering evidence, invalidation, costs, exposure assumptions, historical support/absence and main event risk. A stop threshold is not guaranteed execution or a maximum possible loss.

Approval requires data readiness, research completion, cross-validation, backtest validation, strategy review and final audit. Missing critical domains, invalid times or unresolved severe contradictions block `APPROVED`. A rejection is allowed to end research; it never requires manufacturing another signal.
