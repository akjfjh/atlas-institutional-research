# Research record and report interpretation

The engine's Pydantic models and exported `schemas/` JSON schemas are authoritative for machine validation. Each record has a unique Research ID, input configuration, data snapshot/provenance, validated role-result versions, messages, review issues, state transitions, final decision and generated report locations.

Allowed research decisions are BULLISH, BEARISH, NEUTRAL, WAIT_FOR_CONFIRMATION and INSUFFICIENT_DATA. They are research conclusions, not order instructions. Workflow state and market decision are separate. Only APPROVED qualifies as an approved committee conclusion. An INSUFFICIENT_DATA report must retain visible missing domains even if its available technical analysis is directional.

Role output includes research_id, agent_id, asset, instrument_id, task, timeframes, data_sources, data_timestamps, evidence, findings, market_regime, bullish_arguments, bearish_arguments, uncertainties, contradictions, confidence, requests_to_other_agents, status and version. Evidence references need resolved IDs and source/time provenance. Revision history identifies the original result and correction reason.

Schema 1.1 adds `language` and an explicit `required_roles` list to each research record. Full research requires ten roles, including `btc_specialist` and `eth_specialist`; quick research requires only `technical`. Archived 1.0 records retain their original eight-role requirement when loaded and audited. Specialist results include a reproducible `payload.quant` derived from confirmed, aligned BTC and ETH UTC daily closes of matching market type and quote currency. Audit recomputes the numeric payload from raw snapshot evidence and flags divergence.

Schema 1.3 extends full research to twelve roles by adding `entry_timing` after technical research, with 15m and 1H closed-bar input. It stores `trader_level`, `holding_horizon` and `strategy_mode`; the 1 USDT high-leverage option remains a simulation with `execution_allowed: false`. Schema 1.2 introduced `risk_control` between specialist/backtest research and strategy/chief review. Its `payload.assessment` uses the frozen snapshot and `budget.risk_policy`; audit recomputes the assessment and veto. `prior_research_id` and `prior_observation` retain a previous report's key levels for a new-ID reassessment without mutating either snapshot. Existing records keep their saved role scope. The final market/entry section is a timestamped view of the completed record and becomes stale after publication.

For bilingual output, each result can provide `summary_en`, `uncertainties_en` and `contradictions_en`, and each finding can provide `claim_en`. Host agents should submit faithful English fields alongside Chinese originals. Missing translation is reported as a quality warning; the English view uses a source pointer rather than inventing a translation. Every completed run writes `report.zh.md`, `report.en.md`, `report.zh.html`, `report.en.html`, `market.zh.svg`, `market.en.svg` and `report.json`; `report.md` and `report.html` follow the requested `--lang zh|en`. Exported JSON keeps the original full state and evidence.

Markdown, JSON and HTML exports must expose source timing, market type, freshness, supporting and opposing findings, audit status, historical limitations and execution engine. A score or confidence label cannot be rendered as an uncalibrated percentage win probability. Archive a cached/offline label and never overwrite it with a current-looking report timestamp.
