# Changelog

## 0.6.0 — 2026-10-26

Dashboard and CLI research now require a bounded, read-only Codex CLI review of frozen evidence before a report is complete. Codex writes bilingual conclusions for every role and a market synthesis, with separate plain-language beginner and detailed experienced versions; indicator computation, evidence validation and the final entry gate remain independently checked. Codex failure does not fall back to a parameter-only report. This is one model review of the role questions, not twelve independent model agents. The research-note field is now sent as a question to Codex. Quick scans explicitly report that a full risk officer review did not run.

## 0.5.0 — 2026-10-26

Added a 15m/1H entry-timing role, a one-hour chart, holding-horizon and trader-experience controls, concise bilingual role reports, live role activity, stronger watch/entry chart lines and a conditional setup alert. The 1 USDT 50/100x perpetual view is a read-only simulation with no liquidation-price claim or order endpoint.

## 0.4.0 — 2026-10-26

Added a loopback-only local dashboard with an animated research landing page, live OKX spot/swap/expiry instrument picker, role progress and evidence cards, confirmed-candle charts, risk gate, bilingual report viewer, key-level reassessment and local interaction log. The web mode runs the deterministic research graph and never places orders. Expiry futures remain outside validated rollover/margin backtests and the executable risk sizing gate.

## 0.3.0 — 2026-10-26

Added local first-use profile setup, an independent risk-control role, read-only entry veto and conditional order sizing, timestamped end-of-report market decisions, and new-ID reassessment of prior support/resistance levels. The reported capital is a planning amount, not an exchange-balance read. No live order or proven trading edge is claimed.

## 0.2.0 — 2026-10-26

Added dedicated BTC and ETH research roles with independently sourced matching-market daily data, relative-strength/volatility measures and recomputation gates. Completed runs now write both Chinese and English Markdown/HTML reports. Existing 0.1.0 records retain their original eight-role scope. No trading advantage or live-order capability is claimed.

## 0.1.0 — 2026-10-26

Initial ATLAS research-engine release. Includes structured role orchestration,
read-only data adapters, technical analysis, reproducible backtesting, event
studies, audit, local records and Agent Skills packaging. Actual verification
and incomplete capabilities are documented in `docs/validation-report.md` and
`docs/acceptance-matrix.md`; this changelog does not claim marketplace approval
or a measured improvement in profitability.
